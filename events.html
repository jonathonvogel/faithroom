Hands-On Formation for Your Community

When you invite Faith Room to work hands-on with your team or community (in person or virtually) through talks, retreats, courses and workshops, they will get formed in their faith and equipped with the tools to thrive in life and ministry.

Every event is customized to meet your community where they are. We take time to understand your specific context, challenges, and goals — then build an experience that speaks directly to those needs.

  • Team Faith-Formation Days that align your leadership around our Catholic faith
  • Parish Missions that reignite the spiritual life of your entire community
  • Ministry Sprints that help you get a new initiative off the ground
  • Custom retreats for specific groups — youth, young adults, parents, couples
  • Workshops on charisms, prayer, evangelization, and spiritual growth

Perfect for Ministry Leaders

This option is ideal for any ministry leader — such as a Parish Priest or School Principal — who wants their entire team or community to grow together and get aligned around our Catholic faith.

You don't have to fake it until you make it. Faith Room will give you the support you need to embrace the mission and vocation God has called you to and give you the tools you need to know you're doing it God's way.

We've worked with:

  • Parish pastoral councils and leadership teams
  • School faculties and administration
  • Diocesan departments and ministry teams
  • Catholic organizations and apostolates
  • Knights of Columbus and Catholic Women's League

For Schools

Faith-formation days, retreats, and workshops designed for Catholic school communities — from students to faculty.

Forming the Next Generation

Catholic schools have a unique mission: to form students not just academically, but spiritually. Faith Room partners with schools to create transformative experiences that help students and staff encounter Christ in a real and personal way.

Our school programs are age-appropriate, engaging, and grounded in solid Catholic teaching. We don't just talk at students — we invite them into an experience of faith that they can feel, respond to, and carry with them.

  • Student retreats that meet young people where they are
  • Faculty and staff faith-formation days
  • School-wide Eucharistic Adoration experiences
  • Workshops on charisms, prayer, and spiritual growth
  • Music ministry and worship leading training
♱

Who We Serve in Schools

We work with every level of the school community, recognizing that formation happens best when everyone is growing together.

  • Principals and Administrators: Casting vision and building culture rooted in Catholic identity
  • Teachers and Educational Assistants: Equipping educators to live out their vocation in the classroom
  • Department Heads: Integrating faith across every subject area
  • Campus Ministry Teams: Training student leaders and building vibrant faith cultures
  • Students: Retreats and experiences that invite genuine encounter with Christ

Option 4

Get Ministry Coaching, Feedback and Guidance

Are you encountering a block in your spiritual life or feeling stuck about moving God's plan for your life forward? We are here to help.

Personalized Support You Can Trust

Sometimes you need more than a program — you need a person. Someone who has walked the road before you and can offer honest feedback, practical guidance, and spiritual discernment.

Faith Room coaching is built on the belief that you don't have to figure everything out alone. Whether you're wrestling with a decision, trying to get a ministry off the ground, or feeling stuck in your spiritual life — we're here to walk with you.

  • One-on-one coaching sessions tailored to your specific situation
  • Practical feedback on ministry plans, events, and initiatives
  • Spiritual direction rooted in Catholic tradition
  • Help discerning God's voice in big decisions
  • Accountability and encouragement for the long haul
♱

What Kind of Help Do You Need?

Every coaching relationship starts with a conversation. We'll listen to where you are, what you're facing, and what kind of support would be most helpful. Here are some common areas we help with:

  • Spiritual Stuckness: Feeling like you're going through the motions but not growing
  • Ministry Struggles: Your ministry isn't thriving and you need a second opinion
  • Event Planning: You're planning a faith-based event and want experienced feedback
  • Leadership Challenges: Leading others is harder than you expected
  • Vocational Discernment: You're trying to understand what God is asking of you next
  • Burnout Prevention: You're giving everything and running on empty
✝

Perfect for Anyone Who Wants to Serve God but Feels Stuck

This is for anyone who wants to serve God but is feeling stuck in any way and needs an experienced second opinion. You don't need to have everything figured out — you just need to be willing to take the next step.

You don't have to fake it until you make it. Faith Room will give you the support you need to embrace the mission and vocation God has called you to and give you the tools you need to know you're doing it God's way.

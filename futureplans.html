Future Plans

God is doing something new. Here's a glimpse of what's ahead for Faith Room.

Faith Room is on a mission to be a steadfast companion for missionary hearts everywhere. As we look to the future, we're excited about the ways God is leading us to expand our reach and deepen our impact.

We are continually developing new workshops, retreat formats, and resources to serve the Catholic community. This includes expanded offerings for schools and parishes, new online resources for those who can't attend in person, and deeper partnerships with dioceses and Catholic organizations.

Stay tuned — and if you'd like to be part of what God is doing through Faith Room, we'd love to hear from you. Your prayers, support, and involvement help make this vision possible.
